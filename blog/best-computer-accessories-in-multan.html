1. Choosing the Right Mouse Pad: Speed vs. Control

A mouse pad is often an overlooked accessory, yet it directly impacts mouse sensor tracking. A high-quality pad provides micro-texture parameters that optical and laser sensors read, preventing cursor skips.

There are two main styles of surfaces:

  • Speed Surfaces: Featuring smooth, low-friction weaves, these pads are designed for swift, sweeping movements. Perfect for fast-paced action games and casual browsing. The Xtrike Me MP-005 Medium Pad is an excellent speed surface option.
  • Control Surfaces: Characterized by heavier, textured weaves, these surfaces generate resistance to help you micro-stop your mouse. Highly favored by tactical FPS players and graphic designers. The Razer Goliathus Control Pad is the prime example.

2. USB Hubs & Workstation Expansions

As laptops become increasingly thinner, physical input ports (such as USB-A and HDMI) are being phased out in favor of Type-C. Managing external monitors, backup storage, and wired mice requires a reliable, multi-port USB Hub. Settle for replica adapters, and you risk power drops, interface lag, or device damage.

We recommend choosing certified Type-C hubs that support USB 3.0 data speeds (up to 5 Gbps) and Power Delivery (PD) pass-through charging.
