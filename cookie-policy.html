Cookie & Privacy Policy

1. Introduction

At The Computer Shop Multan, your privacy is our priority. This Cookie Policy explains how we use cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience on our website.

2. What Are Cookies & Local Storage?

Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit a website. Local Storage is a similar web technology that allows websites to store data directly in your browser. These technologies help the website remember your preferences and actions.

3. How We Use These Technologies

  • Recently Viewed Products: We use your browser's localStorage to securely remember the last few products you have viewed. This data remains on your device and is never transmitted to our servers. It is strictly used to render the "Recently Viewed" section for your convenience.
  • Theme Preferences: We store your preference for Light or Dark mode so the website loads exactly how you like it on your next visit.
  • Analytics: We use Google Analytics to understand how visitors interact with our site. This helps us improve our product offerings and site performance. These cookies collect anonymous usage data.

4. Your Choices

You can choose to disable cookies and clear local storage through your browser settings at any time. Please note that doing so will reset your theme preferences and clear your "Recently Viewed" products history.

5. Contact Us

If you have any questions about our Cookie Policy or how we handle your data, please contact us via WhatsApp or visit our physical store in Multan.
