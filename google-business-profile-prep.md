## 5. GBP Optimization Checklist & Local Citations

Complete these tasks to lock in your local SEO signals:

- [ ] **Verify Location pin:** Open Google Maps and verify that the red physical store pin is dropped accurately on your shop location.
- [ ] **Specify hours:** Set accurate operating hours, and update them promptly for public holidays or Eid.
- [ ] **Add real photos:** Upload high-resolution photos of your store front, interior displays, and actual product stock (unboxing original Xiaomi/Samsung chargers, mouse pads). Avoid stock images.
- [ ] **Add Products to GBP Dashboard:** Manually upload the 10 products with images and direct links back to https://multantech.store/ using the product profiles above.
- [ ] **Submit to local directory listings:** Build citations on local Pakistani business directories using the exact NAP details from Section 1:
    *   YellPakistan.com
    *   Bazaarr.pk
    *   Local.com.pk
    *   YellowPages.com.pk
- [ ] **Integrate Google Map on Site:** Ensure the active Google Map pin is embedded on the `contact-us.html` page to signal direct location matching to crawlers.
