? What M.2 SSD sizes does this enclosure support?

This M.2 SSD enclosure supports all standard NGFF (M.2 SATA) form factors including 2230, 2242, 2260, and 2280. Please note it supports M.2 NGFF (SATA-based) drives only � not NVMe PCIe drives.

Yes. For Smart TVs, any TV with a standard USB interface is compatible. For Android phones, your device must support OTG (On-The-Go) function and provide at least 1.2A output via its USB port. You will need an appropriate OTG cable (sold separately).
