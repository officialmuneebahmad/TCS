Detailed Product Overview

The Xtrike Me GH712 is a premium wired gaming headset designed to deliver a fully immersive audio and communication experience across all major gaming platforms. Whether you're grinding ranked matches on PC or playing console titles on PS5 or Xbox, the GH712 adapts seamlessly.

Powered by 50mm dynamic audio drivers with a sensitivity rating of 114±3dB, the headset reproduces every in-game sound with precision — from the distant footstep of an approaching enemy to the deep rumble of explosions. The wide 20Hz–20KHz frequency range means you never miss critical high or low-frequency audio cues.

The omnidirectional noise-reduction microphone (6.0×5.0mm, sensitivity -40±3dB) captures your voice clearly from all directions while filtering out background noise, ensuring teammates hear you and not your surroundings. The 2.2KΩ microphone impedance ensures clean signal output across all connected platforms.

Aesthetics are not an afterthought here. The GH712 features a stunning RGB running-horse light emission mode, cycling through colors dynamically to complement your gaming setup. The LED power is supplied via a standard USB connection, while audio runs through the universal 3.5mm 4-PIN CTIA jack.
