? Do the AirPods 4 support wireless charging?

Yes, this model of Apple AirPods 4 comes with a wireless charging case that supports Qi-compatible wireless chargers as well as standard USB-C charging.

With ANC enabled, AirPods 4 offer up to 4 hours of listening time on a single charge and up to 20 hours when combined with the charging case. With ANC disabled, you get up to 5 hours of listening time and up to 30 hours with the case.
