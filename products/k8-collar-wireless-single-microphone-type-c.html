Frequently Asked Questions

? I recorded a video, but I can't hear any sound during playback. Why?

Your phone thinks the receiver is a set of headphones. To hear the audio playback from your phone's speakers, you must physically unplug the Type-C receiver from your phone.

? Can I charge my phone while recording?

Yes! The Type-C receiver has a passthrough charging port on its side, allowing you to plug your charger in and keep your phone powered during long recording sessions.
