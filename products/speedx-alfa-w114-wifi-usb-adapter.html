Detailed Product Overview

The Speedx Alfa W114 300Mbps WiFi USB Adapter is designed to give your desktop or laptop instant access to any Wireless-N network. Whether your built-in WiFi is weak, broken, or simply absent, this small plug-and-play dongle provides a powerful wireless upgrade in seconds.

Thanks to the powerful RTL8188FTV-VC chipset and 3dBi external antenna, this adapter is built to receive WiFi signals across larger areas and from greater distances compared to standard flat-antenna adapters. It utilizes MIMO technology to maximize data throughput and CCA (Clear Channel Assessment) technology to automatically detect and avoid busy WiFi channels, giving you a more stable and interference-free connection.
