Detailed Product Overview

The Romoss Sense 8P+ (PHP30 Pro) is a high-capacity 30000mAh portable power station built for heavy users — travelers, gamers, students, and professionals who cannot afford to run out of battery. With enough power to keep a smartphone charged for up to a week, it eliminates the anxiety of low-battery warnings.

Its standout feature is 18W Two-Way Fast Charging using both Power Delivery (PD) and Quick Charge (QC) technologies. This means not only can it fast-charge your phone up to 80% in just 35 minutes, but the power bank itself can be fully recharged in approximately 11 hours with an 18W adapter — significantly faster than standard power banks.

Unlike most power banks with a single input port, the Sense 8P+ offers 3 input options — Micro-USB, Lightning, and Type-C — giving you maximum flexibility regardless of which cable you have on hand. Three output ports (1× Type-C + 2× USB-A) allow you to simultaneously charge your phone, tablet, and earbuds all at once.

The smart LED digital display shows the exact battery percentage (not just LED dots), letting you plan your usage with accuracy. Built-in intelligent multi-safety protection guards against overheating, overcharging, over-current, over-discharge, and short circuits for complete peace of mind.
