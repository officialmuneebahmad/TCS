Detailed Product Overview

The ONIKUMA K19 RGB Gaming Headset is engineered for gamers, streamers, and multimedia users who demand immersive audio, crystal-clear communication, and multi-platform versatility. Whether you're battling on PS4, grinding ranked on PC, or watching movies on your tablet, the K19 delivers a premium audio experience out of the box — no software or drivers required.

Multi-Platform Compatibility – Plug and Play: The K19 works seamlessly across PS4, PS VITA, PSP, Xbox One (via controller 3.5mm jack), Nintendo Switch (audio mode), Nintendo 3DS, Windows PC, Mac OS, iOS devices, Android smartphones, tablets, and iPads. Simply plug in and enjoy. There is nothing to install.

Dazzling RGB LED Lights for an Enhanced Gaming Atmosphere: The striking RGB lights on the ear cups create a vivid, immersive gaming atmosphere. The LED system is powered by the USB connector, so you get the full light show on any device with a USB port while audio continues through the 3.5mm jack.

Superior 50mm Audio Drivers for Immersive Sound: Powered by advanced 50mm audio drivers combined with cutting-edge acoustic techniques, the K19 delivers simulated surround sound with deep bass, crisp mids, and detailed highs. You'll hear every footstep, gunshot, and environmental cue with pinpoint spatial clarity — giving you the competitive edge you need.

True Noise Cancellation Microphone: The built-in omnidirectional noise-cancelling microphone captures your voice accurately while eliminating unwanted background noise. Whether you're coordinating with teammates or streaming, your voice comes through clean and professional. The inline volume knob and microphone mute button keep you in control without breaking focus.

Ergonomic Design for Extended Comfort: The 1.2-inch thick ear pads are made from a second-generation skin-friendly material that reduces hearing fatigue and provides exceptional softness during long sessions. The adjustable headband accommodates different head sizes while maintaining consistent, pressure-free comfort throughout hours of use.

⚠️ Important Note for PS4 & USB Users The RGB LED lights are powered exclusively via the USB connector. If your PS4 does not have an available USB port, the RGB lights will not activate — however, audio and microphone via the 3.5mm jack remain fully functional.

How to set up on PS4:
1. Insert the 3.5mm jack into the DualShock controller.
2. Long-press the PS4 button to enter System Settings.
3. Go to: Peripheral Machines → Adjust Sound and Devices → Output to Headphones → Select "All Audio".
4. Note: Some PS4 units restrict third-party microphones.

Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Is the ONIKUMA K19 compatible with PS4 and Xbox One?

Yes. For PS4, insert the 3.5mm jack into the controller and enable "All Audio" under Output to Headphones in system settings. For Xbox One, connect via the controller's 3.5mm jack. The USB plug is only for powering the RGB LED lights. Note: Some PS4 systems may restrict third-party microphones.

❓ Does the RGB light work on PS4?

The RGB LED lights are powered exclusively via the USB plug. If your PS4 does not have an available USB port connected to the headset, the RGB lights will not illuminate. However, all audio and microphone functions via the 3.5mm jack remain fully operational.

❓ Does the ONIKUMA K19 require any drivers or software?

No. The ONIKUMA K19 is a fully plug-and-play headset. Simply connect it via the 3.5mm jack (and USB for RGB) to your device and it works immediately — no drivers, software, or configuration required.
