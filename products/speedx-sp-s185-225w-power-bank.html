Detailed Product Overview

Get high-speed energy storage on the go with the **SpeedX SP-S185 Super Fast Charging Power Bank**. Featuring a rugged ABS shell, high-capacity lithium polymer cells, and advanced charging protocols, it keeps your setups active anywhere.

Key Features:

  • Super Fast Charging: 22.5W high-power output with support for Apple PD 20W and Android 22.5W protocols. Charges up to 50% in approximately 30 minutes.
  • Multiple Output Ports: Equipped with a built-in Type-C cable, plus a USB-A port and Type-C port (1A1C) to power three devices simultaneously with no mutual interference.
  • Two-Way Type-C Cable: The built-in heavy-duty Type-C cable works for both output (charging devices) and input (recharging the power bank up to 18W Max).
