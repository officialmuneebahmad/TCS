Frequently Asked Questions

❓ Does the V200 Ultra Smart Watch work with iPhones?

Yes, the V200 Ultra supports both Android 5.0+ and iOS 10.0+ through the WearFit Pro app. You can receive calls, notifications, and track health data on both iPhone and Android smartphones.

❓ Is the V200 Ultra watch truly waterproof?

The V200 Ultra carries an IP68 waterproof rating, meaning it can withstand immersion in up to 1.5 meters of fresh water for up to 30 minutes. It is safe for rain, hand washing, and light swimming, but is not recommended for diving or hot water (shower/sauna) use.

❓ How long does the battery last on the V200 Ultra?

The V200 Ultra features a 220mAh battery offering up to 65 days of standby time and approximately 10 days of regular usage with all features active. It charges wirelessly via the included magnetic wireless charger.

❓ Can I make phone calls directly from the V200 Ultra watch?

Yes. The V200 Ultra supports Bluetooth 5.2 calling. When paired with your smartphone, you can answer, reject, and make calls directly from your wrist using the built-in speaker and microphone.
