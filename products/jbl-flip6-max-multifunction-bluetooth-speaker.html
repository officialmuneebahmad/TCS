Detailed Product Overview

The JBL Flip6 Max Multifunction Bluetooth Speaker Subwoofer brings massive, room-filling sound in a rugged, portable package. Built for maximum utility, this speaker goes anywhere you go.

With an integrated 2.0 dual-channel full-range crossover, it pumps out up to 50W PMPO to keep your parties loud and bass-heavy. Whether you are using the Live Sound Mode or relaxing with standard playback, the Flip6 Max delivers premium acoustic performance.
