Frequently Asked Questions

❓ What is the difference between US Pin and EU Pin?

US Pin features two flat parallel metal pins, while EU Pin features two round metal pins. Both chargers offer identical 25W charging performance and internal safety circuitry.

❓ Do I need a separate adapter plug for Pakistani sockets?

Most multi-sockets in Pakistan accommodate flat US pins directly. If not, a simple, cheap flat-to-round converter plug is recommended.
